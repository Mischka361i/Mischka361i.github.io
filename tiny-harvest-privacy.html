2. General Information About Data Processing

When using the App, certain technical, device, advertising and usage data may be processed automatically. Depending on the user's device and configuration, this may include:

  • Android Advertising ID (AAID), App Set ID and identifiers generated by third-party SDKs
  • Firebase Installation ID and Crashlytics Installation UUID
  • IP address and approximate geographic location derived from the IP address
  • device manufacturer, device model, operating system and version
  • language, region, time zone, network information and App version
  • App usage information, session duration, gameplay events and game progress
  • advertising impressions, interactions, clicks and performance information
  • crash reports, stack traces, diagnostic data and technical error information
  • information about in-app purchases, such as the purchased product and transaction status

We generally do not require users to provide their name, postal address, telephone number or similar directly identifying personal information within the game unless such a feature is explicitly provided.

3. Purposes of Data Processing

  • providing and operating the App and processing in-app purchases
  • analysing how the App is used and improving gameplay and user experience
  • identifying technical problems, detecting and fixing crashes and improving App stability
  • providing contextual and, where permitted, personalised advertisements
  • measuring advertising performance and reach, limiting how often advertisements are shown
  • attribution and measurement of advertising campaigns for the App, including user acquisition tests
  • fraud and abuse prevention, statistical analysis and security
  • compliance with legal and regulatory obligations

4. Legal Basis for Processing

Personal data is processed only where there is an applicable legal basis. Where processing is necessary to provide the App or services requested by the user, processing may be based on Article 6(1)(b) GDPR. Where we request the user's consent, processing is based on Article 6(1)(a) GDPR. Where information is stored on or accessed from the user's device and such access is not strictly necessary, consent is required where provided for by applicable law, including Section 25 of the German Telecommunications Digital Services Data Protection Act (TDDDG).

Where processing is necessary for our legitimate interests or those of a third party, and such interests are not overridden by the user's rights and freedoms, processing may be based on Article 6(1)(f) GDPR. Legitimate interests may include App security, fraud prevention, prevention of abuse, technical error detection, App stability and contextual advertising where legally permitted. Where processing is necessary to comply with a legal obligation, it is based on Article 6(1)(c) GDPR.

5. Google Firebase and Google Analytics for Firebase

The App uses Google Firebase. For users in the European Economic Area, the relevant provider may include Google Ireland Limited, Gordon House, Barrow Street, Dublin 4, Ireland.

We use Google Analytics for Firebase to understand how users interact with the App and to improve the game. It may process App launches and sessions, session duration, device and operating system information, App version, approximate location, language, region, installation and App identifiers, interactions within the App, game progress and gameplay events defined by us. Depending on the configuration, advertising or device identifiers may also be processed. Data is retained according to our configuration and the retention periods provided by Google.

6. Firebase Crashlytics

We use Firebase Crashlytics to detect, analyse and fix crashes and other technical problems. It may process crash and error reports, stack traces, Crashlytics Installation UUID, Firebase Installation ID, device and operating system information, App version, time of a crash, App status at the time of the crash and technical logs. Crashlytics is used for technical diagnostics and is not intended by us to directly identify individual users.

7. Meta / Facebook SDK

The App uses the Facebook SDK provided by Meta Platforms Ireland Limited, Merrion Road, Dublin 4, Ireland (“Meta”). We use it to measure App installs and App activations and to attribute and evaluate advertising campaigns for the App on Meta's platforms, including marketing tests that may be carried out by a publishing partner. The App does not ask users to log in with Facebook.

The Facebook SDK may process App events (such as App installs and activations, and automatically logged App events), the Android Advertising ID, device and operating system information, App version, language, IP address and related technical information. Meta may process this data as an independent controller or as a processor, depending on the processing activity. Meta's privacy policy is available at facebook.com/privacy/policy.

8. Advertising and Advertising Identifiers

The App is financed, among other things, through advertising. We use advertising and mediation services provided by third-party companies. These services may process the Android Advertising ID, a resettable identifier provided by the Android operating system. This information may be used to provide advertisements, measure advertising performance, prevent fraud, limit repeated advertisements and optimise campaigns. Personalised advertising is only used where legally permitted and, where required, after obtaining appropriate consent.
